About SpeechBee

Founded in 2018 by Dr. Emily Parker, SpeechBee has been dedicated to helping children and adults overcome speech and language challenges through evidence-based therapy approaches.

Our Mission

To create a supportive, engaging environment where every individual can develop effective communication skills and gain the confidence to express themselves fully.

Education & Certifications

  • Ph.D. in Speech-Language Pathology, Stanford University
  • Certified by the American Speech-Language-Hearing Association (ASHA)
  • Specialized training in pediatric speech disorders and developmental delays
  • Advanced certification in Augmentative and Alternative Communication (AAC)
